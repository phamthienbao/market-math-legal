Advertising Identifiers (iOS & Android)

The Application uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to deliver and measure advertising. AdMob may access platform-provided advertising identifiers as follows:

  • iOS — Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA): Access to your IDFA is requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt that appears on first launch (after you respond to the GDPR/UMP consent prompt where applicable). If you grant permission, AdMob may use your IDFA to deliver personalized ads and measure ad performance across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline, AdMob serves only non-personalized ads and your IDFA is not associated with cross-app activity. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iOS device.
  • Android — Google Advertising ID (AAID): AdMob may use your AAID for the same advertising purposes. You can reset your AAID, or opt out of personalized advertising, under Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.

In jurisdictions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent before any personalized advertising occurs.
